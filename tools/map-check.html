Map check

data/ drawn over the map artwork. Every settlement pip should sit inside the outline of the shire it was assigned to, and every outline should follow the border drawn under it — a pip in the wrong colour region is a misassignment, and an outline crossing a border is a mistraced shire. This is the check the checksums cannot do. It used to also catch a pip with no printed letter under it; the artwork carries no letters now, and that check belonged to the transcription, which is done.
